# Sample Data

Do not place real survey or customer videos in Git.

Approved fixtures must be:

- synthetic or explicitly sanitized;
- small;
- documented;
- free of faces, registration plates or customer information where practical;
- licensed for repository use;
- accompanied by expected outputs.

Suggested future fixture groups:

```text
tests/fixtures/
├── synthetic_tracks/
├── synthetic_events/
├── tiny_media/
└── expected_exports/
```
